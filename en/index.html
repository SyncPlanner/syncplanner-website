Features

What it actually
does

Deadlines under control
A clear view of what's urgent, what's in progress, and what's already delivered. No more unpleasant surprises.
Track every track
Composition, revision, mix, delivery — each track has its own status. Every project's progress is visible in real time.
Publisher tracking
Contact details, validation rate, average response time, follow-up history: everything about your publishers in one place.
Production stats
Tracks produced, validation rate per publisher, monthly trends. Concrete data to better plan production.
Recycle refused tracks
A refused track isn't dead. Mark it available and transfer it to another project.
100% local & private
All management data stays on the user's computer. No cloud, no third-party server — except when optionally using the AI assistant. Total privacy.
PRO registrations (SACEM, BMI, PRS…)New
Import your works list: SYNC.PLANNER spots released tracks that can't be found and prepares a clear PDF to send to your publisher.
Sending to publishersNew
Submission email ready in one click, MP3 or WAV attached, bulk send of several tracks, and the full send / follow-up / reply history on each track.
Stems checkNew
Before delivery: missing Main, forgotten stem, offset start or mismatched format are flagged automatically. Plus an automatic daily backup.
Automatic Logic Pro integration
Open a project in Logic Pro: SYNC.PLANNER detects it on its own, picks up the BPM, key and time signature, and tracks the time spent on each track (automatically paused after 10 minutes of inactivity). Renamed a project? It offers to relink it in one click. No manual entry.
SACEM revenue & €/hour profitability
Import your SACEM statements: they're automatically matched to your tracks, with revenue by album and by publisher, plus the time you composed in Logic Pro to work out your profitability per hour. A BMI (US performing rights) integration is under consideration for composers also active on the US market.
🎁 Bonus — Built-in AI assistant (Beta)
Connect your own API key (OpenAI, Claude or Mistral) to get an AI assistant right inside SYNC.PLANNER. It can draw on your projects to help you prioritize production, analyze deadlines, prepare deliveries and answer your questions.
Why SYNC.PLANNER

Built for your
sync composer workflow

For sync music

SYNC.PLANNER is a project management tool for sync music composers — library, film, TV, video games and advertising. Unlike generic tools like Notion or Trello, it's built around the real workflow of a professional composer: albums, tracks, publishers, deadlines and follow-ups.

Your data stays with you

Your projects stay stored locally on your Mac, with nothing passing through our servers — and the app works even offline.

Automatic follow-up tracking

Publishers can take weeks or months to respond. SYNC.PLANNER automatically monitors your follow-ups — per album and per track. When the deadline is exceeded, an alert appears and a professional follow-up email is ready, with your signature and full context — just one click to send it.

An optional AI assistant

SYNC.PLANNER works perfectly without AI: deadline alerts and track tracking are already automated natively, and follow-ups are flagged for you at the right time. The AI assistant remains an optional extra, for anyone who wants to go further.

Who built SYNC.PLANNER?

I'm Joeffrey Longagnani, a library music composer for TV, trailers and documentaries. At first everything was fine — just a few tracks to manage. But as my catalogue grew month after month, I lost track of it all. I searched online for a suitable tool and found nothing comparable, so I built my own app. The result: I always know where I stand. Publisher follow-ups are almost automatic, and one click shows me when I last followed up and whether the publisher replied. No more digging through emails or Dropbox to find a brief: the link is right there in the app. I hope it helps other composers in the same situation!
